# Pipelines

This is [where](toshy://onboarding/pipeline-tab) you create graph topologies for Toshy to execute. It's a visual editor where you add cards and connect them.

I tried to make it as simple as possible, grouping more complex functionality into individual nodes or their settings so you don't have to unravel a puzzle of hundreds of nodes, as you sometimes do in other agent systems.

For detailed information about the editor and its available nodes, [click the button](toshy://onboarding/pipeline-help) with the book icon on the floating vertical toolbar at the top right (inside the Pipelines workspace).
